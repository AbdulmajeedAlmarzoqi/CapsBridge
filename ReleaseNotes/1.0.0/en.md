### CapsBridge 1.0.0

CapsBridge lets you use Caps Lock as the screen reader modifier key in Windows virtual machines in VMPal.

- Sets up your Mac and Windows with a single button.
- Turns on Caps Lock as an NVDA modifier key when NVDA is installed.
- Checks the configuration each time you open it, and resolves issues automatically.
- Removes everything it added, including in Windows.
- Checks for updates and installs them for you.
- Available in English and Arabic.
